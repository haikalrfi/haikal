About

I'm a fresh informatics graduate focused on data analysis. My final project took 12.5 million raw IMDb records down to a clean dataset of 8,340 films, then built and evaluated a recommendation model in Python and MySQL.

What I enjoy is the unglamorous part: cleaning data, checking assumptions, and finding the signal that actually separates one thing from another. On CINEMATCH that meant proving genre barely distinguishes films, so the plot synopsis does most of the work.

Outside coursework, I've spent two years on ticketing and crew at large concerts, from Blackpink to NCT Dream and Dream Theater. That's where I learned to stay calm and precise when thousands of people are waiting on you.

Final-year data project

CINEMATCH

A web app that recommends similar films from their genre, plot, and rating. An audit of the data showed genre alone barely separates titles (a film shares its exact genre set with 99 others on average), so I weighted the plot synopsis to do most of the work, then corrected ratings so low-vote films couldn't game the ranking.
